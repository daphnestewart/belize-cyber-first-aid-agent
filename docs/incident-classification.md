# Incident Classification Framework

## Severity Levels

### Low

- Single user affected
- No sensitive information involved
- Minimal business impact

### Medium

- Multiple users affected
- Potential credential compromise
- Limited operational impact

### High

- Sensitive data exposed
- Unauthorized access confirmed
- Significant business impact

### Critical

- Ransomware
- Large-scale data breach
- Business operations disrupted

---

## Incident Types

| Incident Type | Typical Severity |
|--------------|------------------|
| Phishing | Medium |
| Malware | Medium |
| Unauthorized Access | High |
| Data Breach | High |
| Ransomware | Critical |
| Lost Device | Medium |
| Business Email Compromise | High |

---

## Classification Questions

1. What happened?
2. What systems are affected?
3. Is personal or business data involved?
4. Is the business still operational?
5. How many users are impacted?
6. Is the incident contained?

---

## Decision Process

Identify → Assess Impact → Assign Severity → Respond → Recover
